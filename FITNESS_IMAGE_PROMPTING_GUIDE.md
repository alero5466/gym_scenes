# 🎬 Fitness & Gym Image Prompting Guide

### Complete Image Prompting Guide (Modern CrossFit / Athletic Vibe)

---

## THE RULES (read first)

- Always say **"male"** or **"female"** — no age or nationality at the opening.
- Specify orientation early if needed (Vertical for IG, Horizontal for YouTube).
- **Skin realism block** always goes near the end, before the camera spec. For fitness, this includes sweat and athletic glow.
- **Camera always last**.
- Keep background descriptions **simple** — emphasize the "modern sleek CrossFit box" elements (matte black rigs, clean floors, bright windows) without over-cluttering.
- Always **attach a reference image** for character consistency.

---

## PROMPT ANATOMY

[ORIENTATION / ASPECT RATIO]
[SHOT TYPE + CAMERA ANGLE]
[SUBJECT + ACTION + EXPRESSION]
[CLOTHING — texture specific]
[ENVIRONMENT — simple, only key details]
[LIGHTING — describe behavior not equipment]
[SKIN REALISM & SWEAT BLOCK]
[CAMERA SPEC]

---

## I. SHOT TYPES & ANGLES

### The Mirror Selfie POV
A straight-on shot facing a large clean gym mirror, the subject holding a smartphone at chest level to capture their reflection. The phone partially obscures the lower face.

### Low-Angle Action (Barbell/Dumbbell)
A slightly low-angle shot, camera positioned at knee level shooting upward, capturing the subject mid-lift, emphasizing power and the weight of the equipment.

### Cinematic Waist-Up (Resting / Posing)
A direct front-facing waist-up shot, camera positioned horizontally at chest level. The subject is resting between sets, looking off-camera with a focused, intense expression.

### Product Focus (Drinking Shaker)
A candid medium close-up, camera tracking the subject's face as they tilt their head back slightly to drink from a matte black shaker bottle. The bottle is prominent in the foreground.

---

## II. LIGHTING — DESCRIBE BEHAVIOR NOT EQUIPMENT

Never say "there is a window light" — describe what the light *does*.

### Bright Natural Gym Daylight (Standard)
Abundant natural daylight floods the space from off-camera large industrial windows, filling the room with clean, bright, and even light. The light wraps softly around the subject, creating a natural athletic glow and highlighting muscle definition without harsh, gritty shadows.

### Clean Backlight / Rim Light
Bright natural light pours in from behind the subject, wrapping around their shoulders and arms to create a luminous, glowing rim light. The front of the subject is evenly lit with soft, bouncing daylight, keeping the aesthetic airy and premium.

### Soft Overhead Athletic Glow
Soft, diffused light cascades directly down from high ceilings, catching the sheen of sweat on the skin and casting clean, subtle shadows under the jaw and collarbones to define the physique elegantly.

---

## III. SKIN REALISM & SWEAT BLOCK

Always include this near the end of the prompt. 

### Standard Athletic Realism (opening)
A male with photorealistic skin — visible pores, natural blemishes, a light sheen of athletic sweat, subsurface scattering, individual hair strand detail —

### Full Realism Block (end of prompt)
Ensure facial consistency is identical to the reference image with hyper-realistic skin texture, open pores, and a natural post-workout glow with subtle sweat droplets. Maintain the exact same facial spots, facial hair, and symmetry as the reference image. Muscle definition should look natural and anatomically accurate, not exaggerated.

## V. FULL PROMPT TEMPLATES

### 🏋️‍♂️ Action: Barbell Deadlift/Clean (YouTube Horizontal)
A horizontal wide-angle action shot from a slightly low angle, capturing a male mid-lift, gripping a loaded barbell. He has a focused, intense expression. He wears a fitted, breathable dark grey sleeveless tank top with subtle moisture wicking texture, and athletic shorts. The environment is a modern, sleek, luxury CrossFit box with clean rubber flooring, matte black pull-up rigs in the background, and uncluttered space. Abundant natural daylight floods the space from off-camera large industrial windows, wrapping softly around the subject and highlighting muscle definition without harsh shadows. Ensure facial consistency is identical to the reference image with hyper-realistic skin texture, open pores, and a light sheen of athletic sweat. Maintain the exact same facial spots and symmetry as the reference image. Shot on Sony A7IV, 35mm f/1.8, cinematic depth of field.

### 📱 Social: The Mirror Selfie (Instagram Vertical)
A vertical straight-on shot facing a large clean gym mirror, capturing a male holding an iPhone at chest level to take a reflection selfie. He stands with confident, cinematic posture. He wears a fitted black sleeveless tank top that drapes naturally. The reflection shows a sleek, premium CrossFit box behind him, featuring neatly racked dumbbells and bright natural daylight spilling across the floor. The lighting is clean and airy, providing an even, flattering athletic glow. Ensure facial consistency is identical to the reference image with hyper-realistic skin texture, a natural post-workout glow, and individual hair detail. Shot on iPhone 15 Pro, vertical format, authentic social media aesthetic, photorealistic.

### 🥤 Lifestyle: Drinking from Shaker (Versatile)
A direct front-facing waist-up shot, capturing a male taking a break, drinking from a matte black shaker bottle. He looks relaxed but focused. He wears a sweat-dampened olive green sleeveless tank top. The background is slightly blurred, showing the premium matte black equipment of a modern CrossFit box. Bright natural daylight catches the edge of his shoulders creating a subtle rim light, while soft bounced light fills his face. Ensure facial consistency is identical to the reference image with hyper-realistic skin texture, open pores, natural skin oils, and subtle sweat droplets on the forehead and arms. Shot on Sony A7IV, 85mm f/1.8, soft bokeh background, photorealistic.

### 📸 Cinematic Posing: Dumbbell Rest (Vertical or Horizontal)
A straight-on medium portrait shot, capturing a male sitting casually on a black gym bench, resting his forearms on his knees with heavy dumbbells resting on the floor beside his feet. He looks directly at the camera with a calm, confident expression. He wears a fitted sleeveless tank top. The environment is a clean, modern CrossFit box with a bright, luxurious but functional aesthetic. Soft, diffused daylight cascades down, catching the sheen of sweat on his arms and chest, defining the physique elegantly. Ensure facial consistency is identical to the reference image with hyper-realistic skin texture, open pores, and the same facial hair and symmetry exactly as the reference image. Shot on Sony A7IV, 50mm f/1.4, highly detailed, RAW photograph.

## VI. COMMON GYM FIXES

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Barbells/Weights look bent or melted | Add "perfectly straight barbell, perfectly round weight plates, anatomically correct grip, no warped geometry" |
| Gym looks too gritty/dungeon-like | Add "bright airy atmosphere, sleek modern equipment, clean luxury athletic space" |
| Skin looks plastic/smooth | Add "no smoothed or airbrushed skin, natural athletic sweat sheen, visible skin texture" |
| Image looks like a 3D render | Add "Shot on Sony A7IV, RAW photograph, natural lighting, photorealistic" |
| Subject looks overly muscular/unnatural | Add "natural athletic physique, realistic muscle proportions, not a bodybuilder" |
